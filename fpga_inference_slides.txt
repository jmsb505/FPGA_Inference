Did we achive the
objective?
17
FPGA implementation
Volumetric MR-to-CT registration was successfully
deployed on the FPGA.
The DPU executes the quantized 2.5D network.
The ARM CPU completes flow reconstruction and
volumetric warping.
Axial, coronal and sagittal predictions are fused into a
3D deformation field.
Main Results
The FPGA produces competitive registration quality.
The DPU is faster than ARM-only board execution.
The local GPU remains the fastest platform.
The main FPGA limitation is ARM-side processing, not
DPU inference.

17
Final Conclusion
Volumetric image registration was successfully implemented
on FPGA using Vitis AI.
The 2.5D architecture provided an FPGA-compatible solution
to the original volumetric task.
The FPGA deployment maintains competitive registration
quality with a compact INT8 model.
DPU acceleration reduces board runtime compared with
ARM-only execution.
The local GPU remains significantly faster.
ARM-side flow reconstruction and volumetric warping are
now the main performance bottlenecks.
